## 💡 Why Dual Theme Support is Essential:

1. ☀️ **Light Mode (Porcelain White `#F8F9FA`):**
   - Ideal for **bright daylight & heavy shop tube lights** at mobile counter desks. High contrast dark text on crisp white cards prevents glare under direct shop lights.
2. 🌙 **Dark Mode (Deep Obsidian `#0B0E14`):**
   - Ideal for **low light / nighttime operations**, reducing battery consumption on OLED screens and eye strain.
3. 🔄 **Auto-System Sync:**
   - App automatically switches themes based on phone system preferences or manual 1-tap toggle in `Settings ⚙️`.
